# Elite Golem Affix Bases Pass 8

Pass 8 keeps the pass7 front-facing cropped elite golem silhouettes, enlarges the head/face read point, and adds stronger emissive material reads for the three non-red base affixes.

## Direction

- `armorSpore`: larger face plus green bioluminescent pits and curved organic glow veins inside the spore armor.
- `jump`: larger face plus brighter blue propulsion material on the shoulder fins and hip thrusters.
- `haste`: larger face plus stronger yellow-gold airflow channels and conductive chest cracks.
- `berserk`: larger face/eyes while preserving pass7 red crystal and rage core language.
